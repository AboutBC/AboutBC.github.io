<p>
I'm an <strong>economist and policy researcher</strong> with experience working in
<strong>the White House</strong> across two administrations.
My work focuses on the evaluation of public programs and policies, with an emphasis on
political economy, criminal justice, and applied microeconomic research.
</p>

<p>
I previously served as a <strong>Pathways Economist</strong> at the
<em>Office of Management and Budget (OMB)</em> in <strong>the White House</strong>.
Before that, I was an <strong>Analyst Intern</strong> at the
<em>U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)</em>.
My current research examines <strong>firearm violence</strong>,
<strong>gang-related crime</strong>, and the effectiveness of
<strong>criminal justice and youth intervention programs</strong>.
My broader academic research includes the causal effects of
<strong>gun policies</strong>, <strong>voting rights protections</strong>,
and <strong>drug testing policies</strong> for TANF and SNAP.
</p>

<p>
I have experience in <strong>R</strong>, <strong>Python</strong>, and <strong>Stata</strong>.
</p>
