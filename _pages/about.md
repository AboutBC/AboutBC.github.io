I'm a policy researcher and economist with experience across federal and academic settings. My work focuses on the evaluation of public programs and policies, with an projects in political economy and applied microeconomic policy analysis.

Previously, I served as a Pathways Economist at the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) and as a Analyst-Intern at the U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO). My academic work focuses on examining the causal impacts of gun policies, voting rights protections, and drug testing policies on TANF and SNAP.

I have experience in R, Python, and Stata.
