<p>
I’m a <strong>policy researcher and economist</strong> with experience across federal and academic settings. My work focuses on the evaluation of public programs and policies, with projects in political economy and applied microeconomic policy analysis.
</p>

<p>
Previously, I served as a <strong>Pathways Economist</strong> at the <em>Office of Management and Budget (OMB)</em> and as an <strong>Analyst Intern</strong> at the <em>U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)</em>. My academic research examines the causal impacts of <strong>gun policies</strong>, <strong>voting rights protections</strong>, and <strong>drug testing policies</strong> on <strong>TANF</strong> and <strong>SNAP</strong>.
</p>

<p>
I have experience in <strong>R</strong>, <strong>Python</strong>, and <strong>Stata</strong>, and build reproducible, data-driven workflows for both policy and academic audiences.
</p>
